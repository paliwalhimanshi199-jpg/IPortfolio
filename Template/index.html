Dataset

EV Records & Specifications

Base layer

Cleaned registration and spec data

Manufacturer, manufacturing country, category and record counts form the core of every chart. These fields feed the three dashboard filters, so any slice of the data can be isolated interactively.

Derived Measures

Calculated

Share, ranking and aggregation

Counts are rolled up per category and per manufacturer, with ranking used to surface the leaders. Aggregation stays dynamic so the numbers respond to whatever filters a visitor selects.

What The Data Shows

Four angles the dashboard is built to answer. Open the dashboard and the filters move every number below.

Market Distribution By Country

Manufacturing country is one of the three dashboard filters. Selecting a country re-scores every category and manufacturer in the view, so concentration becomes immediately visible.

Open dashboard

Category Performance

The ranked bar chart orders categories by record count. It is the fastest way to see which segments dominate the dataset and how wide the gap is between leader and tail.

Open dashboard

Manufacturer Spread

With 200 manufacturers and roughly 18,000 models in the data, the manufacturer filter is the tool for isolating a single maker and comparing its footprint against the rest of the market.

How To Read This

The two views answer different questions. Here is which one to open, and what each control does.

01

Start With The KPI Cards

Total manufacturers, total models and annual sales set the scale. Every chart below them is a breakdown of those totals.

02

Narrow With The Filters

Country, manufacturer and category each cut the whole dashboard at once. Combine them to isolate a single slice, then clear them to return to the full market.

03

Read The Ranked Bars

The category chart is ordered, so position is the message. Look at the top of the list for the leaders and the length of the tail for how fragmented the market is.

04

Switch To The Story

The story takes the same numbers and presents them in sequence. Use it when you want the narrative rather than free exploration.

05

Hover For Detail

Tooltips give exact values behind each mark, so you can read precise figures without estimating from bar heights.

06

Open It Full Screen

Every card has an "Open in Tableau" link. Use it on a small screen, or to get the full toolbar and download options.
